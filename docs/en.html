Byzantine music, step by step, on your phone

An Android app for learning to read and chant: theory, the 8 modes with an ison drone, recording and analysis of your own voice, a weekly mode calendar and a digital lectern. The app is available in Greek and English.

Download the APK from GitHub Coming soon to Google Play

Android 7.0 or newer. Free and open source. All releases and changes

What is inside

Everything works on your device, offline.

Your data stays on your device

The app has no internet permission, asks for no account and contains no ads or analytics. The microphone is used only when you start it, and audio is processed only on your phone. This page uses no cookies or analytics either.
